Privacy policy

Updated October 23, 2026.

This policy covers Tripeiro for Android, its Wear OS, Android Auto and widget features, the Tripeiro Plus subscription, shared trips and this website. The person responsible is Gerson Donscoi Junior. For privacy questions and requests, contact donscoijr@gmail.com.

Tripeiro organizes trips without requiring an account. Information you enter is stored in the app's storage. Only people who share a trip sign in with their Google account, and only the shared trip and how long Plus lasts reach our server. This does not prevent transmissions by the services described below: maps, address search, metrics, diagnostics, subscription, shared trips, system backups and watch synchronization.

Trips and files on your device

Trips, destinations, dates, transport, bookings, stays, saved contacts, activities, places, coordinates, notes and checklists are stored in a local database. The app does not read your address book or request the device's current location. Saved coordinates describe places in your itinerary.

When you import a PDF or image as an attachment, the app keeps a copy in its private storage, together with its name, type and size. Deleting the copy in the app does not delete the original. Themes, language and other preferences are also stored on the device. Reading and editing this information does not require internet access; maps and address searches may need a connection.

Backups and device transfers

In versions that provide access to this policy through the menu, the database and preferences may be included in Android cloud backups, subject to your settings and system limits. Attachment copies are excluded from these backups. Restoring the database does not recover those files: you will need to import them again. Attachment metadata, such as names and types, is part of the database.

On Android 12 or later, direct device transfers allow the database, preferences and attachments to be transferred. Earlier systems may also exclude attachments from transfers because they use legacy backup rules. Execution depends on Android and the manufacturer; a complete copy is not guaranteed. Earlier test versions may have different rules. The watch app declares backups disabled and receives its data through phone synchronization. Tripeiro's developer does not have access to your Android backup.

Maps and address searches

Maps use the Google Maps SDK. Displaying and moving a map involves requests for the areas and places shown. Google also receives technical data such as IP addresses, an SDK-specific identifier, device information, interactions and diagnostics. Address searches use Android's Geocoder and send the search text to the geocoding service available on the device. These features do not amount to tracking your current location.

When you choose to open a place or route in Google Maps or another app, the necessary locations are passed to that app. See the Google Privacy Policy and Maps SDK information.

Usage metrics and diagnostics

The phone distribution build uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand usage and Firebase Crashlytics to investigate failures. Collection is active in that build, with no menu option to turn it off. Development builds disable collection by default and may enable it for testing. The watch app does not include these SDKs.

Custom events report actions and fixed categories, such as opening a screen, creating a trip or importing a file type. They do not include trip text, booking codes, addresses, coordinates, or attachment names or content. Analytics also processes automatic events, an app-instance identifier, device data and approximate region derived from IP addresses. The app disables advertising ID, SSAID and ad-personalization signals in its configuration.

Crashlytics receives technical reports, installation and session identifiers, device information and app state. Handled errors sent by the app omit the original message and causes. Automatic fatal crash reports may include the exception message; we cannot guarantee that diagnostics never contain incidental information. We do not intentionally add trip content to reports.

Analytics is configured to retain user and event data for 14 months, without resetting the user identifier's retention period on new activity. This period does not apply to standard aggregated reports. According to Google, Crashlytics reports and associated identifiers are kept for 90 days before removal from live and backup systems begins. Other service operational data follows the provider's terms. See Firebase privacy information and Analytics retention information.

Watch, car and widget

Wear OS receives a summary of ongoing and upcoming trips, including transport, stays and dated itinerary items. It may include notes, coordinates and booking codes in details. Attachment files are not included. Synchronization uses the Google Play services Data Layer, which may use Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or Google infrastructure to connect your devices. The watch keeps the latest received summary while waiting for updates.

Android Auto and the widget display summaries for viewing and do not display booking codes. Deleting or changing a trip on the phone updates those summaries when processing and connectivity allow. A disconnected device may keep an older copy until it synchronizes. With Tripeiro Plus, the widget has a button that opens the expense form on the phone, and the watch adds an expense (amount and category), sent to the phone through the Data Layer; without the phone nearby, the expense is kept on the watch until it is sent. Otherwise, trips cannot be edited on the watch, car or widget.

Tripeiro Plus (subscription)

Tripeiro Plus is a monthly subscription sold through Google Play. Payment is handled by Google Play; Tripeiro does not receive or store your card or payment details. No feature that already existed became paid.

To know whether the subscription is active, the app uses the RevenueCat service, which receives the subscription's purchase history from Google Play (product, dates, renewals, cancellations and the purchase token) and links it to an app identifier. That identifier is random until you sign in with your Google account to share a trip; after that, it becomes your Firebase account identifier, so the server knows until when your Plus lasts. RevenueCat does not receive your name or email. It also receives technical request data such as the IP address, app and system versions, language, store country and currency. This data is used to turn Plus on, restore the purchase and show the subscription status. Trips and expenses are not sent to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

The last answer is kept on the device so Plus works offline until it expires. RevenueCat tells our server about subscription changes, and the server keeps only until when Plus lasts, tied to the account of the person who shares. Cancelling or letting the subscription expire does not delete trips: expenses remain available to view. The subscription is managed and cancelled on Google Play.

Shared trips and Google account

With Plus, the owner of a trip can share it by invite. To do so, they sign in with their Google account through Firebase Authentication, which keeps the name, email and profile photo provided by Google and an account identifier. Local trips keep working without signing in.

The shared trip is sent to Firebase Cloud Firestore, in Tripeiro's project: name, destination and dates, itinerary, notes, stays, legs (including carrier, number, seat and booking code), saved contacts, coordinates, checklists, budget, travelers (the names the owner wrote) and expenses with their split. Attachments and photos are not sent. The invite keeps the trip name and the invited traveler's name.

Guests do not sign in: the app creates an anonymous Firebase identifier for them, tied to the invite and to the traveler chosen by the owner. Guests receive the trip to view and, while the owner's Plus lasts, can add and edit their own expenses, which go back to the server and to the owner. Anyone who receives a trip can copy what they see; revoking the invite or deleting the trip removes it from the guest's device on the next sync. If the owner's Plus ends, guests keep the copy they already received.

When Tripeiro is installed from an invite link, the app reads the invite code through Google Play's Play Install Referrer API. To protect the server, Firebase App Check uses Google's Play Integrity API to attest that requests come from the original app. The invite and account deletion pages are on Firebase Hosting, which may process IP addresses and access logs.

Deleting your account

In the app, tap ⋮ on the home screen, then Account and Delete account. Without access to the app, write to donscoijr@gmail.com from the email of the Google account used in Tripeiro; we reply and delete it within 30 days. The steps are also at travel-memory-6b1af.web.app/excluir-conta.

Deletion removes from the server the account (name, email and photo), the trips it shared with their records, the invites, the guests' devices tied to them and how long Plus lasts as kept on the server. Guests stop receiving those trips. The trips on your phone stay there. Deletion does not cancel the subscription: cancel it on Google Play. Google Play's and RevenueCat's purchase records follow their own policies; to ask for your RevenueCat history to be deleted, write to the email above.

Exports, links, AI and support

Saving or sharing a PDF itinerary passes the exported information to your chosen destination, including embedded structured data for reimport; attachment files are not embedded. The latest shared export may remain temporarily in private cache until another export or Android cleanup. Copies saved outside the app or received by other people are not erased when you delete a trip.

Opening links, phone numbers, email or WhatsApp passes the necessary address or contact to your chosen app. AI planning takes place in the tool you choose, outside Tripeiro. The app reads the result you paste locally and does not connect to your AI account. Those tools have their own policies.

If you contact us by email, we process the information you send to reply and follow up on your request. GitHub reports are public, except for the private vulnerability channel. Do not send real tickets, bookings or documents. You may request deletion of support information under our control, subject to support needs and applicable obligations.

Website, providers and security

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not receive itineraries or use the app's Firebase Analytics. The policy page works without JavaScript or login. The main website selects a language from the link or browser language; GitHub may process IP addresses and access logs to operate and protect its hosting under its privacy statement.

Google, RevenueCat, GitHub, the geocoding provider and your chosen apps may process information in other countries, such as the United States, under their terms. Firestore and Tripeiro's server functions are in Google Cloud's São Paulo region. Local data uses Android storage isolation; the SDKs mentioned use protected connections, and access to Firestore is limited by rules: only a trip's owner and guests can read it. Exported PDFs do not receive a password or Tripeiro-specific encryption. Protect access to your devices, backups and shared files. No system can offer absolute security.

Retention, deletion and your requests

Local data remains until you delete it or clear app data. Uninstalling removes Android-managed local storage but does not automatically erase backups, exports, information already received by third parties, or watch copies that have not yet updated. Manage backups in Android settings and external copies at their destinations.

Deleting a trip removes its related records and requests cleanup of internal attachment copies. If file cleanup fails, the app tries again at startup. The developer does not hold a central copy of trips that were not shared. A shared trip stays on the server while the owner's Plus lasts. Deleting the trip in the app erases its content from the server; only a record that it was deleted remains, with no name or items, so guests remove it from their devices. Items deleted inside the trip also lose their content on the server. That record and any shared trip are erased from the server 90 days after the owner's Plus ends, or earlier, when the account is deleted. The subscription status kept on the device stays until the next check or uninstall. Deleting local data does not automatically erase telemetry already sent. For questions or requests to access, correct or delete information under our control, contact donscoijr@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request; technical identifiers do not always allow a report to be associated with the person writing to us.

Tripeiro is intended for adults. Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new update date.
