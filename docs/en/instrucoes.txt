# Tripeiro — plan trips and prepare them for import

English translation of the Tripeiro skill 1.0.2, format `tripeiro-plan` version 1. [Português](https://github.com/gdonscoi/tripeiro/blob/main/skills/tripeiro/SKILL.md).

Tripeiro is a personal, offline Android app for organizing trips: transport legs, stays, daily itineraries (activities, notes and checklists) and contacts. The person talks with you to plan, then pastes your result into the app using **⋮ → Import from AI** or the **Plan with your AI → I have the result** card on the home screen. The app checks the block, shows a review and creates **a new trip**, which they can edit afterward.

You have no access to the app or the person’s account: the only transfer is the block they copy. Talk in the person’s language.

## Two paths

1. **Plan from scratch.** Ask only for missing, relevant information: destination, dates (or duration), travelers, pace and preferences, transport and stays already booked. Do not start a long questionnaire; propose and adjust.
2. **Use an existing conversation or itinerary.** Use what has already been discussed in this conversation or pasted by the person. Ask only about gaps that prevent a coherent result (for example, trip dates to distribute activities by day).

## Honesty rules

- **Never invent** booking references, reservation codes, flight numbers, seats, phone numbers, emails, links or coordinates. Include only what the person provided or confirmed; for addresses and coordinates, also use trustworthy sources verified as described below.
- Suggestions (sights, times, restaurants, routes) are suggestions: make that clear in the conversation and, when useful, in `notes` (for example, "Suggestion: check opening hours"). Do not present a suggestion as a confirmed booking.
- Whenever the format accepts `address`, `latitude` and `longitude` (stays and activities), research the place’s full address and coordinates using available search tools and trustworthy sources. Reuse supplied data and confirm that the address and coordinates match the correct establishment or point of interest; ask for clarification if ambiguous. Include the address in `address` and numeric coordinates in `latitude` and `longitude`, always together. Do not substitute the city center for the place. If you cannot verify data or cannot search, omit unverified fields and briefly disclose the limitation in the response; keep confirmed data. Do not add these fields to types that do not accept them.
- Times follow the ticket (local time, without a time zone). For a car without an agreed time, use only the date — never `00:00`.
- Data without a dedicated field (price, rating, attachment links from the conversation) is summarized in `notes` or left out. Attachments and images from the conversation are **not** transferred to the app; tell the person if they are relying on them.

## When the person asks for the result

Typical request: "Prepare this itinerary to import into Tripeiro using the skill."

1. Read [the format](formato-do-roteiro.md) and follow it exactly: fields, types, allowed values and what each type does **not** accept.
2. Build **one single** `json` code block with the complete object (`"format": "tripeiro-plan"`, `"version": 1`). See the [fictional example](exemplo-roteiro.json).
3. Before delivering, check:
   - Valid JSON, without comments or trailing commas; no fields outside the format.
   - Dates `YYYY-MM-DD`; transport `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM` (car may use date only); `endDate` ≥ `startDate`; `checkOut` ≥ `checkIn`.
   - `OTHER` has `modeName`; other modes do not. `CAR` has no `company`, `number`, `seat` or `booking`.
   - `NOTE` has only `type`, `day` and `notes`; `CHECKLIST` has `items` (≥ 1) and optionally `title` and `day`; `ACTIVITY` has `title`.
   - Every `day` is within `startDate`…`endDate` (and the trip has both dates).
   - Addresses and coordinates were researched for stays and activities; included data matches the correct place, coordinates come together and are within bounds, and any research limitations were disclosed.
   - Contacts have real, valid values (phone with ≥ 8 digits, preferably with `+country code`).
4. After the block, in one or two lines, say to copy the whole block and paste it into Tripeiro (**⋮ → Import from AI** or **Plan with your AI → I have the result** on the home screen), that every import creates a new trip and that **"You can change and fine-tune it whenever you like in the app."**

If the trip exceeds the format’s limits (for example, more than 500 items), explain and suggest splitting it into separate trips.

## If the app refuses the result

The app has a **Copy correction request** button, and the person may paste the request into the conversation. It includes the problem, location (for example, `Where: transports › 2 › departure · "Porto Alegre → Gramado"`, with lists counted from 1 and the item’s name) and a format hint. Fix **only** the reported problem and keep everything else. If the request says the app could not read the result, review the whole block: one single block, valid JSON and correct `"format"`/`"version"`. Always deliver the **complete** block again, never just the corrected excerpt, because the person replaces the old block with the new one in the app. The format’s "Common errors" table helps identify the cause.

# Tripeiro itinerary format — `tripeiro-plan` version 1

Reference for the block the AI gives the person to paste into Tripeiro (⋮ menu → **Import from AI**). The app checks everything on the phone, offline, shows the trip for review and only then creates **a new trip**. No existing trip is changed.

This format is independent of the app’s database and Tripeiro PDF package. It has no IDs or internal metadata: the app creates IDs when saving.

## How to deliver

- One single `json` code block containing one complete JSON object. Text before or after the block is ignored.
- Do not split the result across blocks: the app refuses more than one code block.
- Valid JSON: double quotes, no comments, no comma after the last item.
- Dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`; times as `HH:MM` (24-hour); transport date and time as `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`, using the ticket’s local time without a time zone.
- Unknown information = **omit the field**. `null` and `""` also count as absent; never fill a value just to avoid leaving it empty.
- Fields the app does not know, or that the item type does not use, cause the app to **refuse** the result (nothing is silently discarded). Extra information goes in `notes`.

## Structure

```json
{
  "format": "tripeiro-plan",
  "version": 1,
  "trip": { ... },
  "transports": [ ... ],
  "stays": [ ... ],
  "itinerary": [ ... ],
  "contacts": [ ... ]
}
```

`format`, `version` and `trip` are required. Lists are optional; each list’s order is the order displayed in the app.

### `trip` — the trip

| Field | Required | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `name` | yes | Trip name, up to 200 characters. |
| `destination` | yes | Main destination, up to 200 characters. |
| `startDate` | no | `YYYY-MM-DD`. |
| `endDate` | no | `YYYY-MM-DD`, not before `startDate`. |
| `notes` | no | Free text, up to 10,000 characters. Links, emails and phone numbers in the text become tappable in the app. |

### `transports` — transport legs (up to 100)

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `mode` | Required: `FLIGHT`, `BUS`, `TRAIN`, `CAR`, `BOAT` or `OTHER`. |
| `modeName` | Required **only** for `OTHER` (for example, `"Transfer"`); forbidden for other modes. |
| `origin`, `destination` | Required, up to 200 characters. |
| `departure`, `arrival` | Optional. `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`. Only `CAR` accepts a date without time (`YYYY-MM-DD`). |
| `company` | Carrier (airline, bus company…). Not used for `CAR`. |
| `number` | Flight or service number. Not used for `CAR`. |
| `seat` | Seat. Not used for `CAR`. |
| `booking` | Booking reference or reservation code. Not used for `CAR`. Never invent it. |
| `notes` | Free text. |

Car: if no time has been agreed, use only the date. **Never** use `00:00` to mean "no time".

### `stays` — accommodation (up to 100)

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `name` | Required, up to 200 characters. |
| `address` | Up to 500 characters. |
| `checkIn`, `checkOut` | `YYYY-MM-DD`; `checkOut` not before `checkIn`. |
| `booking` | Reservation code, only if provided by the person. |
| `notes` | Free text. |
| `latitude`, `longitude` | Numbers, always together (latitude −90 to 90, longitude −180 to 180). Research and verify the place’s coordinates as instructed by the skill; a written address does not replace this numeric pair. |

### `itinerary` — activities, notes and checklists (up to 500)

| Field | ACTIVITY | NOTE | CHECKLIST |
|---|---|---|---|
| `type` | required | required | required |
| `day` | optional | optional | optional |
| `title` | **required**, up to 200 | — | optional, up to 200 |
| `time` | optional, `HH:MM` | — | — |
| `address` | optional, up to 500 | — | — |
| `notes` | optional, up to 10,000 | **required** (the note’s text) | — |
| `latitude`, `longitude` | optional, together | — | — |
| `items` | — | — | **required**, 1 to 200 items |

`—` = the field does not exist for this type and must not be sent.

- `day`: `YYYY-MM-DD` within the trip period (`startDate` to `endDate`). Without `day`, the item appears under "No day set". Items with `day` require a trip with start and end dates.
- For stays and activities, research and verify the full address (`address`) and numeric `latitude`/`longitude` pair as instructed by the skill. These are separate fields: include verified data and omit what cannot be confirmed. Latitude −90 to 90 and longitude −180 to 180, always together.
- Order within each day follows list order.
- Each checklist item: `{ "text": "...", "done": false }`; `text` required (up to 200 characters), `done` optional (default `false`).

### `contacts` — contacts and links (up to 100)

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `kind` | Required: `LINK`, `PHONE` or `EMAIL`. |
| `name` | Required, up to 200 characters (for example, "Hotel reception"). |
| `value` | Required. `LINK`: web address with a domain (`https://` is added if absent). `PHONE`: at least 8 digits; only digits, `+`, spaces, parentheses and hyphens; use the country code (`+55…`). `EMAIL`: `name@domain.com`. |
| `notes` | Free text. |

Only include contacts provided by the person or from a source they confirmed. Do not invent phone numbers, emails or links.

## Limits

Up to 200,000 characters in pasted text and JSON depth up to 10. Lists: 100 transport legs, 100 stays, 500 itinerary items, 200 items per checklist, 100 contacts.

## What does not go into the app

- Attachments, images and files from the conversation.
- Prices, ratings and other data without a dedicated field: if useful, summarize in `notes`.

## Compatibility

| Format | Tripeiro app |
|---|---|
| `tripeiro-plan` version 1 (skill 1.0.0 to 1.0.2) | Tripeiro with the **Import from AI** menu. |

A newer format version is refused by older apps, with guidance to update the app.

## Common errors

| The app says | Likely cause | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Incomplete or damaged result | Partially copied block, trailing comma, JSON comment | Generate and copy the whole block again. |
| More than one code block | Result split into parts | Deliver everything in one block. |
| Field Tripeiro does not know | Invented field (`price`, `rating`…) | Remove or move to `notes`. |
| Field not used by this type | `company` in `CAR`, `title` in `NOTE`, `modeName` outside `OTHER` | Remove or move to `notes`. |
| Invalid or missing time | A non-car leg with only a date | Provide the time or omit `departure`/`arrival`. |
| Day outside trip dates | `day` before start, after end, or trip without dates | Adjust the day, trip dates or omit `day`. |
